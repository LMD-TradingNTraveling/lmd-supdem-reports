Reporting Methodology

Tradovate actuals are authoritative. Research data supplies context and is labeled according to source. Historical current-logic Strategy Analyzer runs are not treated as exact replays of the then-live ATS.

Trading Day

Futures trading-day convention is used throughout the research workflow. Calendar displays use Sunday as the first column.

Research

Observe → accumulate evidence → independently test → deploy only after validation.

Failed Winner Watch

Tracks losing trades reaching ≥1.0R MFE and evaluates historical outcomes against current 1R / Stop-Hunt management when a valid counterfactual is available.

Filename standard going forward

Monthly: LMD_SupDem_MMYYYY.html   ·   Weekly: LMD_SupDem_MMYYYY_wkN.html

Existing August and September monthly filenames remain linked as uploaded. New reports use the shortened standard.
